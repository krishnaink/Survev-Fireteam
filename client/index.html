    What's New!

    September 10th, 2026

    Fireteam 1.7 — Final official release

    We have finally made an official Discord community for Survev Fireteam players. Please feel free to join: discord.gg/w2MDPCpYqC.

    This build also ships team leaderboards, map-targeted support strikes, mobile shop fixes, daily database backups, and cleaner local/dev matchmaking.

    September 6th, 2026

    Hey Everyone!

    I wanted to let you know that we've just released a new update to the game! However at a cost, we've had to remove the old leaderboards because they were being spammed with fake scores that attempted to tamper with the leaderboard system. We've had to shutdown and patch the game to prevent this from continuing. We thank you for your understanding and patience.

    September 6th, 2026

    Fireteam 1.6

    Big quality pass for Spec Ops. Pick a live server region from the dropdown, play with a controller / Steam Deck, and dial in new graphics quality presets when older hardware needs room to breathe.

    Hold for a throwable radial, enjoy menu music on the start screen, and reconnect cleaner if you drop mid-match. Leaderboards now remember which Fireteam version set the score.

    September 5th, 2026

    On Contract

    Mid-wave Spec Ops contracts are clearer and stick around longer. Hold zones show a real stand area on the map and in the world — no more guessing the circle.

    Contracts last 5 waves, then a 4-wave break before the next offer. The retrieve / upload disk job is gone; keep hunting hold packages, Most Wanted, and weapon cash multipliers.

    September 5th, 2026

    Hold the Line

    Spec Ops on phones just got a lot cleaner. The landscape HUD no longer fights itself — meds and ammo sit next to the minimap, weapons stay clear of the emote button, and you can tap the wave timer to lock the lobby.

    Dropped connections now try to auto-rejoin, join spam and proxy IP issues are dialed back, and waves no longer hard-stop at 30. Keep pushing.

    September 3rd, 2026

    Full Roster

    Fireteams can now run up to 8 operators with a full teammate HUD. Create Game is less cramped on desktop and mobile, and that mobile rate-limit kick when opening a lobby should be gone.

    The stats page is Spec Ops only, social links match the main menu, and branding is officially Survev Fireteam.

    August 20th, 2026

    Fireteam Deployed

    Spec Ops is live — a wave survival mode for fireteams. Clear escalating AI hostiles, earn cash between rounds, and gear up at buy stations for weapons, armor, perks, and support.

    Create or join a lobby, revive fallen teammates, and climb the rounds survived leaderboards. Welcome to Survev Fireteam.

    August 9th, 2026

    Summer Breeze

    After a harsh stretch of airstrikes and summer heat, the foliage has risen and Woods has returned to the island! Rush to grab some devastating 12 guage weapons or crown some new kings.

    For those who want to get up to speed with the latest leeks, news, and updates, be sure to check out our Discord Server! or our wiki!

    July 15th, 2026

    Breaking the Bank

    As the island aridifies, enterprising survevrs may notice that a new monetary institution has risen out of the sands: The Reserve. A massive bank constructed to hide PARMA's best kept secrets, the vault is rumoured to contain exotic and powerful weaponry.

    For survevrs trudging through the desert heat, find respite, resources, and fresh water in the oasis. Or, join the hunt for the last supplies sent to the front during the Incursion, and try to find out why a familiar club's insignia keeps on appearing on these damn airdrops...

    Of course, no bank heist is complete without big guns. The Department of Advanced Weaponry's newest hat trick of the S&W 500 revolver, the ASh-12 assault rifle, and the Barrett M107 anti-materiel rifle are all capable of dishing out massive damage, but be sure to conserve your precious .50 cal ammo.

    June 19th, 2026

    The Eleventh Hour

    As we continue to work on our next big update, enjoy another round of 50v50 along with a requested change: Lone Survevrs can now ping all allies, regardless of their original squads!

    June 1st, 2026

    Tuber Trouble

    With the augmenting vats having exhausted PARMA's reasources, focus has shifted to the FSTMS division and further testing of the PMG-134.

    Our next large update is making steady progress; for all the latest leeks, be sure to join our Discord Server!

    May 11th, 2026

    Precursor

    Before the Soviet incursion and brutal fighting that ensued, PARMA scientists were given a directive: create the strongest survevr they possibly could. Their work now lies deep within the Twins...

    Project: COBALT has returned! Along with much-needed adjustments for the Medic and Demo classes, PARMA is proud to announce a new perk: Indomitable Spirit, allowing survevrs to shrug off fatal injuries through (quite literally) pure adrenaline.

    Additionally, the long-anticipated Survevr Pass 1 has arrived, allowing you to flex your style with over 20 different cosmetics! The pass is completely free; just log in and complete two quests per day! If you don't like a quest, you can refresh it for a new one.

    April 13th, 2026

    IntElegance

    As both sides exhaust their nightshade and weapon supplies, the Island rests again... what could have prompted such fierce fighting?

    Over the past couple of years, PARMA has relied on third-party archives to train newbie survevrs, many of which were either misleading or hard to navigate. We've taken matters into our own hands and begun developing a comprehensive guide: the Survev.io Wiki!

    However, many of the sections are still yet to be filled, so we're welcoming our veterans to help contribute! For easier communication between survevrs, be sure to join the Discord Server!

    April 1st, 2026

    Potato vs. Tomato

    While survevrs have since become accustomed to the periodical spuddy incursions on the island, life was not always this way. Once, botanist brothers Spud and Percy Solanum worked in tandem, seeking to uncover the mysteries of the [REDACTED]. In an act of betrayal, the Soviets obtained their precious research secrets, fragmenting their partnership forever. Join the fight of potato vs tomato... and may the best nightshade win!

    Additionally, PARMA's FSTMS division is proud to present their latest concoction: the PMG-134 (Potato Minigun 134), using a six-barrel rotary machine gun to blast tater tots at insane firerates. It's perfect for mowing down your local Soviet!

    March 24th, 2026

    Spring Blossoms

    As PARMA continues to work on their research, the snow has melted and spring has returned to the island! Enjoy the cherry blossoms!

    For the latest leeks and developments, be sure to join the Discord Server!

    March 9th, 2026

    Snow, Smoke, & Shells

    As we head into the final stretch of winter, our favourite arboreal mode has returned with a fresh coat of snow! For those who have been fighting through the winter weather, seek refuge at the campfires spread out across the island.

    In addition, PARMA's Department of Advanced Weaponry would be proud to present the new SPAS-16, capable of unloading heavy damage... if they could find where their prototype went, that is.

    The standard-issue IMD-2 is great for surpressing enemies with its high fire rate, but will have you asking your squad for ammo more often than not.

    As PARMA attempts to defrost their simulation equipment, those who persist will be rewarded with the first blossoms of spring.

    February 2nd, 2026

    High-Speed Hunting

    A new structure, lootable perks, and an open map: welcome back to the Savannah hallucination! For the brave, the bold, and the bloodthirsty, a secondary 'perk' is given to the kill leader: The Hunted, revealing your position to all enemies.

    Ever wanted your bullets to travel at absurd speeds? Look no further than PARMA's new perk: High-Velocity Rounds! This perk greatly increases bullet speed & range.

    Additionally, a new warehouse has been constructed, offering a more dangerous secure place to store valuables, such as the new construction case.

    If you need hunting partners, look no further than our Discord Server!
